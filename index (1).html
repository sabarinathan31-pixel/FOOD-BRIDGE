Safety Standards & Compliance

Food Donation Begins With Responsibility.

Food donation should always be handled with care. FoodBridge encourages donors and receiving organizations to share and review accurate information about food preparation, storage and collection.

Preparation Time

Know when the food was prepared with exact timestamps published directly by donors.

Storage Information

Understand the required storage conditions (ambient, refrigerated, frozen) for integrity.

Allergen Details

Review known allergen information and dietary specifications prior to requesting.

Collection Deadline

Follow the stated safe-use and collection deadline to ensure meal freshness.

Safety Notice: FoodBridge is a coordination platform and does not automatically certify food as safe for consumption. Users must follow applicable food-safety requirements, including relevant FSSAI regulations.
